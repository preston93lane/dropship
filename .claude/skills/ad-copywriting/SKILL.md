# Ad copywriting

Ad copy supports the creative; it rarely rescues a weak one. Its jobs: give the scroller one reason to tap, pre-qualify buyers, and stay honest.

## Platform specs (verify periodically)

| Field | Meta (FB/IG) | TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| Primary text | ~125 chars visible before "more"; up to ~2,200 | Ad text 1-100 chars (keep ≤ 80) |
| Headline | ≤ 40 chars (≈27 shown on mobile) | (n/a: display name + CTA button) |
| CTA button | Shop Now / Learn More / Get Offer | Shop Now / Learn More / Order Now |

## Formulas

- **Short (hook + benefit + CTA)**: "Your desk is 6 cables away from clean. Magnetic organizer snaps on in seconds. Free US shipping."
- **Problem-agitate-solve**: problem in their words → why the usual fixes fail → the mechanism → offer.
- **Story** (for longer Meta primary text): first-person *brand* voice ("We got tired of…"), never a fake customer.
- **List**: "3 reasons desks look messy (and the 10-second fix)".
- **Headlines**: benefit + specificity ("Tidy desk in 10 seconds"), offer ("2-pack: save 18%"), curiosity ("The zip-tie replacement").

## Rules of thumb

- The first line must work alone. Lead with the outcome or the pain, not the brand.
- Concrete beats clever: numbers, seconds, counts, objects.
- One promise per ad; it must match the video/static and the landing page.
- Emojis: 0-2, purposeful (✓ for lists). No ALL CAPS sentences.
- Write 3 primary texts (short, medium, story) and 5 headlines per angle; the media-buyer tests them as variations.

## Compliance checklist (fix before handing off)

- [ ] Every factual claim is supported by product facts (dimensions, materials, what's included, real test).
- [ ] No health, medical, weight-loss, financial or guaranteed-results claims.
- [ ] No "#1", "best-selling", "doctor recommended" or "as seen on TV" without proof we can show.
- [ ] No personal-attribute targeting ("Are you overweight?", "Struggling with anxiety?"). Describe the situation instead.
- [ ] No fake urgency or scarcity; discounts reference real prices.
- [ ] Reviews and testimonials are real and quoted accurately; no invented customers.
- [ ] Shipping time and guarantee match the store's policy and the supplier's reality.
- [ ] No competitor trademarks used in a disparaging or confusing way.
