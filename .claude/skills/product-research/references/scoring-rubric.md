# Scoring rubric (0-10 per component)

Score with evidence, not feel. If you can't support a component, leave it unset (the product stays "watch").
`margin` and `fulfillment` are computed by the gateway from the economics and the supplier.

| Component | 0-2 | 3-5 | 6-8 | 9-10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **demand** | Flat/declining interest, few views | Some interest, niche-only | Rising interest, millions of views across creators in 30d | Rising sharply across platforms and search, early in the curve |
| **proof** | No one advertising it | 1-2 advertisers, short-lived ads | 3+ advertisers or an ad live 30+ days with variations | Many advertisers scaling (many variations, long-running) + sales data |
| **wow** | Needs explanation | Useful but not visual | Clear demo: problem → solution in <5s | Jaw-drop moment in the first second; comments say "need" |
| **creative_potential** | One angle only | 2 angles, similar formats | 3-5 distinct angles across formats (demo, UGC, comparison, gift) | 6+ angles, multiple avatars, natural UGC and motion-graphic formats |
| **competition** (10 = open) | Saturated: dozens of stores, price war | Crowded but differentiable | Some competitors; unclaimed angles exist | Few competitors, clear white space |
| **trend_timing** (10 = early) | Peaked/declining | Mature plateau | Growing for 2-6 weeks | Just starting, early adopters only |

Verdicts (computed): `reject` (hard gate failed) · `watch` (<55 or missing evidence) · `validate` (55-72) · `priority` (≥72).
The self-improver calibrates component **weights** weekly from real outcomes; the anchors above stay fixed so scores stay comparable.
