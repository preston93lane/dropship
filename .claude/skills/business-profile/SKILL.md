## Economics

- Price band: $24.99 - $69.99; target AOV ≥ $40 via 2-packs/bundles
- Minimum contribution per order before ads: $15; minimum markup 2.5x landed cost (see unit-economics)
- Max shipping time promised: supplier's real time + 2 days; prefer US warehouses (≤ 7 days)

## Hard exclusions (never research, list or advertise)

Weapons and weapon accessories; drugs, vapes and paraphernalia; adult products; supplements, ingestibles and cosmetics making efficacy claims; medical devices or anything needing medical claims; children's car seats or safety-critical baby gear; recalled products; branded, licensed or trademarked designs and knockoffs; counterfeit-prone categories (designer goods, electronics brands); items with batteries > 100Wh or hazmat shipping; financial or "get rich" products.
