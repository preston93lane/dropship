# First products and year-one forecast

*Researched 2026-11-25. Prices and rankings change weekly, so re-check them before you order samples. Supplier
costs are **estimates**: supplier sites block automated price checks, so getting real quotes is step 1 (§10).
Benchmarks came from web research the same day; open a source before relying on any single number.*

## The short version

- **Niche: dog owners' home and travel gear.** These are $60-100 problem-solvers that show what they do in one
  shot on video. Dog owners already pay these prices at Chewy, Walmart and on TikTok Shop.
- **First three products to test:**
  1. **Large (10 L) stainless-steel dog water fountain**, $89.99 with spare filters. Strongest evidence: a large dog
     fountain was the top-selling pet item on TikTok Shop in October 2026.
  2. **Hard-bottom back-seat extender** for dogs, $64.99. Strong visual demo, no electronics.
  3. **Dog grooming vacuum kit**, $99.99. Test it in February, ahead of spring shedding, and only from a supplier with
     electrical certification documents.
  - The **paw cleaner cup** is an add-on ($19.99 in the cart, winter-walk bundle), not an ad product: it fails the margin gate on its own.
- **When:** set up in December and launch the first tests the week of **January 4, 2027**. Meta CPMs were about
  $28 in November and about $17 in January ([Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/facebook-ads-cost-per-impression)).
- **Money:** bring **$5,000 of risk capital you can afford to lose. $3,000 is the minimum.** Finding one real winner
  typically takes $2,500-4,000 of testing, and the extra $2,000 is the biggest single lever in the forecast.
- **Months 1-2:** expect to be down about **$1,600** (January about -$1,050 including setup, February about -$550). Revenue will be $1,000-3,500 a month, but at a loss.
- **Year one (base case, $5,000):** 87% chance of finding a real winner and 65% chance of a profitable year. The outcomes
  split sharply. **13%** lose about $4,800 and stop. The **typical winning store** does about $220k revenue and about $14k net.
  The **top 10%** do $840k+ revenue and $100k+ net. Most dropshippers fail (1-20% are profitable in year one, by
  different counts), so **plan your cash for the conservative case** and run the business to hit the base case.

## 1. Why dog owners' home and travel gear

- **Big, steady demand.** US pet spending was $158B in 2025, projected $165B in 2026, across about 95M pet-owning
  households ([APPA](https://americanpetproducts.org/news/u.s.-pet-industry-reaches-158-billion-in-2025-poised-for-continued-growth-in-2026),
  [APPA](https://americanpetproducts.org/news/the-american-pet-products-association-appa-releases-2025-state-of-the-industry-report)).
- **Buyers already pay our price.** Comparable fountains, seat extenders and grooming kits sell for $60-120 at Chewy,
  Walmart, Best Buy and Target (comps in §2). On TikTok Shop a large dog fountain sold an estimated 20,978 units at
  $119.99 ([Nolyn](https://www.nolyn.ai/case-studies/trending-pet-products)).
- **Made for video.** Every product shows a before and after on an object, not a body: slimy bowl vs flowing filtered
  water, dog sliding into the footwell vs a flat platform, hair everywhere vs hair in the canister. That is what the creative system is built to make.
- **Clean compliance.** No health or weight claims needed, no sizing to a body, no licensed designs.
- **The risk to respect:** owners are getting more price-conscious (22% spent less on pets in 2025,
  [Pet Age](https://www.petage.com/appa-report-pet-industry-consumer-spending/)), and big-box stores sell similar
  items with fast shipping. Every offer needs a visible reason to buy from us (bundle, warranty, better spec), and the
  research loop must keep the `soldCheaperAtBigBox` check honest.

**Why not cheap viral toys?** Puzzle and chew toys move volume (Nocciola puzzle toys sell 6,000+ units a month,
[Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/tiktok-hot-selling-2026-dog-toys)), but they're anchored at about $10-28. At that
price, contribution per order can't pay a realistic paid-social CPA (gate: $15 minimum). They suit TikTok Shop
creator/affiliate sales later, not cold ads now.

## 3. Unit economics

Shopify Payments 2.9% + $0.30, 5% refund allowance, $0.50 other per order. "Landed" is product plus shipping to the
customer from a US warehouse, at **estimated** low / mid / high costs. Computed with `core/economics.ts` (`npm run economics`).

| Fountain @ $89.99 | $30 / $38 / $46 | $52.08 / $44.08 / $36.08 | same as contribution | 1.73 / 2.04 / 2.49 | $36.46 / $30.86 / $25.26 | pass / pass / **fail** (markup 1.96x) |
| Seat extender @ $64.99 | $20 / $25 / $31 | $39.06 / $34.06 / $28.06 | | 1.66 / 1.91 / 2.32 | $27.34 / $23.84 / $19.64 | pass / pass / **fail** (markup 2.1x) |
| Grooming vacuum @ $99.99 | $36 / $43 / $52 | $55.29 / $48.29 / $39.29 | | 1.81 / 2.07 / 2.54 | $38.70 / $33.80 / $27.50 | pass / pass / **fail** (markup 1.92x) |
| Paw cleaner @ $24.99 | $6 / $9 / $12 | $16.22 / $13.22 / $10.22 | | 1.54 / 1.89 / 2.45 | | pass / **fail** / **fail** |

**Walk-away landed costs**, the most each product can cost delivered and still pass: fountain **$45** (at $89.99) or $40 (at $79.99);
extender **$29** (at $64.99); vacuum **$50** (at $99.99) or $42.50 (at $84.99); paw cleaner $7 standalone.

**One rule changed for this research:** the margin gate used to demand a 2.5x markup on everything, which rejected all
three mid-ticket products. Paid social needs *dollars* of room per order, and a $90 product at 2.3x has far more CPA
room ($44) than a $25 product at 3x ($13). Products earning **≥ $30 contribution per order** now need 2.0x markup.
Everything else, including the $15 minimum contribution and 35% minimum margin, is unchanged (`DEFAULT_MARGIN_GATES` in
`core/economics.ts`; remove `highTicketContribution` to go back to the strict rule).

## 4. What a winning test has to look like

Cost per sale = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR) ÷ conversion rate. Against the fountain's $44 breakeven:

| | CPM | Link CTR | CPC | Conversion | CPA | vs breakeven |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weak test (most tests) | $17 | 1.2% | $1.42 | 0.8% | $177 | 4.0×: killed in days |
| New store, typical paid social | $17 | 1.8% | $0.94 | 1.0% | $94 | 2.1×: loses money |
| Established-brand median (Triple Whale) | $15.06 | 2.39% | $0.63 | 1.53% | $41 | 0.94×: about breakeven |
| **A winner** | $15 | 2.8% | $0.54 | 2.2% | **$24** | 0.55×: scale |

The benchmark medians come from established brands with repeat customers. A new store's paid-social traffic
converts at about 0.8-1.2% ([Red Stag](https://redstagfulfillment.com/average-conversion-rate-for-shopify-stores/),
[Instant](https://instant.so/blog/average-shopify-conversion-rate)). **To win you need a hook well above median CTR and a
page and offer converting about 2%.** That's why creative quality and the product page are where the system spends its effort.

### What the store looks like after a year: three pictures

1. **No real winner** (13% in the base case, 49% conservative). The stop-loss shuts testing down somewhere in months 3-6,
   about $4,800 down including setup. You keep the system, the playbook, the creative library and the lessons, all

2. **One solid winner at a time (the typical success).** About $220k revenue, $82k ad spend and **$14k net** for the

3. **Top 10%: several winners.** $840k+ revenue, $100k+ net, about $110k a month by December. The next moves are Scale

## 9. Rules that protect your money

1. **Platform caps first.** Set the Meta account spending limit to your remaining test budget; fal credits prepaid with no auto top-up.
2. **At most 3 products testing at once** until the first winner.
3. **Don't override the guardian's kills.** A near-miss gets one new-angle round, never just more budget.
4. **Probation before scaling.** A new winner runs one more week at test budget, which catches the 1-in-3 false positive.
5. **End-of-February checkpoint.** If more than half the capital is gone and no product has held CPA under breakeven for
   7 days, cut to 2 tests a month and fix creative and the page, or switch niche. Don't add money mid-stream without a new plan.
6. **Hard stop.** With no live winner and under $300 left, stop. The forecast assumes you do.
7. **Judge net, not ROAS.** Use the console's 7-day P&L and `/pnl`.

## 11. How the forecast works, and its limits

```bash
npm run forecast -- --scenario all --capital 5000      # or --scenario base --capital 3000 --runs 10000 --json
```

- **What it simulates:** 52 weeks × 5,000 runs per scenario (`scripts/forecast.ts`). Each product test draws a true
  CPA as a multiple of breakeven (lognormal; medians 1.7 / 2.0 / 2.3× breakeven, so typical test ROAS is about 0.9-1.2 against a
  breakeven ROAS of about 2.05). It adds measurement noise (a few sales can mislead), one new-angle round for near-misses,
  a probation week for winners, and scaling at +35% a week with diminishing returns until average CPA reaches 0.78× breakeven.
  Winners fade 4-6% a month, CPA is seasonal (November +12%, December +8%), machine and tool costs apply, and testing
  stops when cash falls under $300 with no winner.
- **Not modeled** (risks that push toward conservative): ad-account bans, supplier failures, return spikes, chargebacks.
- **Not modeled** (upside): filter re-orders and email, TikTok Shop and affiliates, real UGC creators.
- **Taxes:** all figures are before income tax.
- **Re-calibrate:** after 10-15 real tests, set `rMedian` to your actual CPA-to-breakeven ratios and re-run. Your data beats any benchmark.
